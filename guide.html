Getting started

The first time Bill Compass opens it asks whether you would like reminders, then points out the two things you need to find: the + button for adding a bill, and the Calendar. After your first bill is added it shows you, once, how to record a payment.

On iPhone, the five main screens — Overview, Calendar, History, Reports and Tags — are tabs along the bottom. Archived and Settings are in the ••• menu at the top left. On iPad and Mac every screen is in the sidebar, with each of your tags listed beneath.

Adding a bill

  1. Tap the round + button in the bottom corner of the Overview or the Calendar. From the Calendar, the day you have selected is filled in as the due date.
  2. Fill in the bill, then tap Save.

Tip: enter the next due date, not the first one you ever paid. Bill Compass works out every date after it from the frequency.

Acting on a bill

Tapping a bill does nothing, on purpose. You scroll past bills far more often than you act on them, and a tap that opened something would turn every interrupted scroll into a payment screen. Instead:

  • Touch and hold a bill (right-click on a Mac) to open its menu.
  • On iPhone and iPad you can also swipe: swipe right for Pay and Skip, swipe left for Edit, Archive and Delete.
  • On a Mac, double-click a bill to record a payment.

Recording a payment

  1. Touch and hold the bill and choose Record Payment.
  2. Check the amount. Choose Full if this settles the cycle, or Partial if you have paid only part of it.
  3. Set the Payment Date if it was not today, and optionally a Payment Method, a Confirmation Number and a Note.
  4. Save.

A full payment moves the bill to its next due date, and the payment appears in History. A partial payment leaves the bill due, marked with a half-filled circle, until the rest is paid. A one-time bill paid in full is finished.

Paid late or early? Record it with the date you actually paid. The bill still moves on by one cycle.

Skipping a cycle

When a bill was not due this time — a holiday from the gym, a waived fee — touch and hold it and choose Skip This Cycle. It moves on to its next date without money being recorded, and History lists the skip so the gap is explained later.

Automatic payments

Turn on Recorded Automatically for any bill that leaves your account by itself. On its due date Bill Compass records a full payment for you and moves the bill on, exactly as if you had done it by hand. Its reminder becomes a nudge to ensure enough funds are available in the account. Such bills carry a small A beside their name.

A bill whose amount is still zero — a varying bill whose statement has not arrived — is left for you to record, rather than logged as a $0.00 payment.

The Overview

Your bills, soonest first. Each shows its amount and a countdown whose colour says how close it is: red for overdue, then orange and amber as the date approaches, and teal when it is comfortably far off.

Use Category to show one kind of bill, and Due to show Overdue, Current Month, or the Next 30, 60 or 90 Days. The two combine. Show All Bills clears them.

The coloured dot beside the page title is your status at a glance — green when nothing is overdue, amber for one or two overdue bills, red for more.

The Calendar

Days with a bill due are marked. Select a day and the list beneath opens at it, showing that day's bills and everything after. Today returns to the current date, and the arrows beside the month step between months. Bills in the list respond to touch and hold exactly as they do on the Overview.

The Calendar lists only what you still owe. Paid bills are in History.

History

Every payment you have recorded, grouped by month and marked Paid in full, Partial payment or skipped. Use the Payments filter to show one bill's record — or choose Payment History from any bill's menu to jump straight there.

Touch and hold a payment for View Payment, Edit Payment or Delete Payment. Deleting a full payment or a skip also puts the bill back on the date it was due before, so a mistaken payment is undone completely. A skip can be deleted but not edited.

Reports

  • Upcoming — the total of everything falling due in the next 30, 60 and 90 days.
  • Annual Projection — what a year of your recurring bills costs, by category. Tap the ⓘ beside a category to see how its figure is worked out. One-time bills are not counted, and varying bills count once they have a payment to average.
  • Paid This Year — what you have actually paid since January 1, bill by bill, with a total. Archived bills are included, because that money was still spent this year.

Tags

Make a tag from a bill's edit screen (type it in New Tag and press Return), or from the Tags screen's + button. Open a tag to see its bills and their yearly cost. Touch and hold a tag to Rename or Delete it — deleting a tag never deletes its bills.

Archiving a bill

When you stop paying something but want to keep its record, choose Archive from the bill's menu. It leaves the Overview, the Calendar and your reminders, and every payment against it is kept.

Find archived bills under Archived (in the ••• menu on iPhone, in the sidebar on iPad and Mac). From there you can Restore a bill to the Overview, see its Payment History, or delete it for good — which also deletes its payments and cannot be undone.

Reminders

  1. Open Settings and turn on Bill Reminders. Allow notifications when your device asks.
  2. Choose a Time Of Day for reminders to arrive.
  3. Choose how far ahead New Bills Remind you — on the due date, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week or 2 weeks before. This is the starting point for bills you add from now on; each bill keeps its own setting, which you can change on its edit screen.
  4. Turn on Also On The Due Date for a second reminder on the day itself.

The number on the app icon counts bills that are overdue or coming due within your reminder window.

Appearance, reminder times and the start screen are set per device, so your Mac and your phone can differ. Your bills, payments, tags and payment methods are your data and sync with the rest.

Backup and restore

Settings → Backup → Export Backup… writes your bills, payments, tags and payment methods to a single CSV file. Save it to Files, email it to yourself, or keep it anywhere you like.

Restore From Backup… reads that file back. It merges by record — updating what it recognises and adding what is missing — and never deletes anything already in the app.

On the Mac

  • Right-click a bill for its menu; double-click to record a payment.
  • ⌘T jumps the Calendar to today; ⌘[ and ⌘] step between months.
  • ⌥⌘K switches the bill list between standard and compact tiles, to fit more on screen.
  • With sync on, the Sync Now toolbar button asks iCloud for changes straight away.
